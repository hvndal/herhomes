Her Homes Co. — image credits
=============================

The service-chooser panel backgrounds and three of the style-world
images are stock photography from Pexels. The Pexels Licence permits
free commercial use and modification and does not require attribution;
these are recorded anyway so the source of every image on the site is
traceable.

  https://www.pexels.com/license/

These are stock photographs of homes and cleaning in general. They are
NOT photographs of Her Homes Co.'s own work, and the site does not
claim they are. Replace them with real photos of real jobs whenever
those exist.

The scandinavian, bohemian, college-core and minimalist style-world
images were supplied with the project and are not fetched by this
script.
